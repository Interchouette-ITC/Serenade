Email message, Mime lite types, and sync transports (Symfony Mailer shaped).

Types: Email, Address, Body, Attachment. Transports: NullTransport, FileTransport, and (feature smtp) SmtpTransport. DI: RegisterDefaultMailerPass seeds service id DEFAULT_MAILER_SERVICE.
