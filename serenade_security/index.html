AuthN/Z hooks: users, tokens, voters, HTTP firewall, and CSRF.

Full OAuth/OIDC is out of scope. Apps plug bearer or API-key authenticators into FirewallMiddleware. CSRF uses HmacCsrfTokenManager (stateless HMAC). See docs-dev/SECURITY.md.
