HTML form component: bind request, CSRF by default, XSS-safe render helpers.

Apps declare fields, kinds, and constraints; Serenade owns CSRF validation and XSS-safe escaping. Theme helpers (form_row, form_widget, …) and multipart uploads (FieldKind::File) are part of this crate. See docs-dev/FORMS.md.
